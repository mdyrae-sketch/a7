Data Protection & Guest Privacy Governance

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • SunsetTableGo Nomadic Dining Hospitality Atelier

1. Introduction and Governance Framework

SunsetTableGo ("we," "our," "us," or the "Organization") operates the digital portal located at sunsettablego.com, our nomadic table reservation systems, and our bespoke culinary gathering concierge services. We are thoroughly committed to maintaining the confidentiality, digital integrity, and security of all personal data provided by visitors, dining patrons, private event hosts, and culinary community members. This comprehensive Privacy Policy articulates the specific methodologies through which we collect, organize, store, process, transmit, and protect personal records when you interact with our online platform, engage with our interactive Sunset Table Configurator, reserve seats at our communal sunset suppers, or subscribe to our seasonal harvest chronicles.

Our operational practices comply with rigorous international privacy standards, including the European Union General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), the UK Data Protection Act, and applicable United States federal and state data privacy statutes. We encourage you to carefully read this document to understand our handling of personal information.

2. Categories of Information We Collect

In order to deliver bespoke outdoor dining experiences, calibrate seating configurations, accommodate complex dietary needs, and coordinate private pavilion logistics, we collect several categories of information based on your voluntary engagement with our services:

A. Direct Identifiers and Contact Information

When you submit a reservation request, join our newsletter dispatch, or contact our hospitality concierge desk, we collect your full legal name, primary email address, mobile telephone number, billing address, and residential postal details.

B. Dietary Preferences and Culinary Health Details

To safely execute multi-course wood-fired banquets tailored to your physiological requirements, we collect voluntary information regarding food allergies, dietary preferences (such as plant-based, gluten-free, or specific ingredient exclusions), and dining party guest preferences. This data is utilized strictly by our culinary team for safe meal preparation.

C. Digital Device and Usage Telemetry

When browsing our digital repository, our servers automatically record technical telemetry, including your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser software type, operating system version, referring uniform resource locators, screen resolution, time zone configuration, pages viewed, dwell time across individual research articles, and interaction events with our interactive table configurator tools.

D. Transaction and Reservation Records

When booking seats at our nomadic banquets or reserving private dining pavilions, transaction identifiers, itemized course configurations, guest counts, and booking timestamps are retained. Payment processing is conducted through encrypted third-party payment gateways utilizing PCI-DSS Level 1 compliant infrastructure; our servers never store raw sensitive card payment details directly.

3. Lawful Basis and Purposes of Processing

We process your personal records under legitimate legal grounds established in international data governance laws:

  • Contractual Performance: Processing necessary to fulfill dining reservations, prepare custom multi-course menus, coordinate event venue logistics, and deliver hospitality services.
  • Legitimate Interests: Conducting research and development on culinary techniques, optimizing our digital interface, preventing fraudulent bookings, and ensuring network cybersecurity.
  • Explicit Consent: Sending seasonal banquet calendars, farm partnership stories, and direct concierge notifications when you have opted in.
  • Legal Compliance: Retaining mandatory tax ledgers, commercial invoices, and regulatory compliance records as mandated by applicable municipal and trade authorities.

4. Data Minimization and Retention Practices

SunsetTableGo adheres strictly to the principle of data minimization. We only collect the minimal personal data required to achieve specific hospitality, culinary safety, and customer service objectives. Guest dining profiles and dietary preferences are archived securely to facilitate future table reservations until you explicitly request profile deletion.

General transaction and commercial records are retained for a maximum duration of seven (7) years in compliance with international commercial accounting statutes. Server telemetry logs and digital analytics records are anonymized or permanently deleted after twenty-four (24) months.

5. Third-Party Service Providers and Data Transfers

We do not sell, rent, or lease your personal information to third parties. We share data solely with trusted external service providers who assist us in operationalizing our platform under strict non-disclosure and data processing agreements:

  • Hospitality and Venue Partners: Private farm estates, historic orchards, and nature conservancies that require guest manifests for authorized site entry.
  • Cloud Infrastructure and Hosting: Tier-4 data center operators providing encrypted storage, firewall management, and distributed network availability.
  • Analytics and Diagnostic Services: Analytics platforms, such as Google Analytics, that assist us in evaluating aggregate site traffic and user journey optimization.

6. International Data Transfers

Because SunsetTableGo welcomes international culinary travelers, your personal data may be transferred to, and processed in, jurisdictions outside your country of residence, including the United States. When transferring personal data from the European Economic Area (EEA) or United Kingdom, we implement European Commission Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) and robust technical safeguards to ensure equivalent data protection standards.

7. Technical and Organizational Security Measures

We deploy multi-layered defense-in-depth security architectures to safeguard your information against unauthorized access, accidental destruction, alteration, or disclosure. These measures include:

  • 256-bit Transport Layer Security (TLS) encryption across all web traffic.
  • AES-256 encryption at rest for customer databases and guest records.
  • Strict role-based access control (RBAC) limiting data access solely to authorized hospitality and culinary personnel.
  • Continuous vulnerability monitoring, automated threat detection, and regular external code audits.

8. Your Legal Privacy Rights

Depending on your geographic location and local statutory provisions, you possess extensive rights regarding your personal records:

  • Right of Access: You may request a formal copy of all personal records we maintain concerning your identity and dining reservations.
  • Right to Rectification: You may correct inaccurate, outdated, or incomplete personal data.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): You may demand the permanent deletion of your personal records, subject to statutory retention exceptions.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may obtain your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Object or Restrict Processing: You may restrict certain processing activities or opt out of direct marketing dispatches at any time.

To exercise any of these statutory rights, please transmit a formal request to our Data Protection Officer at privacy@sunsettablego.com.

9. Children's Privacy

Our platform and bespoke dining services are directed toward adults, culinary travelers, and private event hosts. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal records from children under the age of sixteen (16). If we become aware that a minor has provided personal information without verified parental consent, we will promptly purge such records from our databases.

10. Updates to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update this Privacy Policy to reflect advancements in our culinary services, modifications to digital service offerings, or evolving international legal mandates. Any amendments will be published on this page with an updated Effective Date. Continued interaction with our website following such revisions signifies acknowledgment of the updated terms.
