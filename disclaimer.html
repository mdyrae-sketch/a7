Safety, Allergens & Environmental Disclaimers

Dining & Outdoor Disclaimer

Important information regarding open-air dining environments, seasonal ingredient variations, and natural terrain safety.

1. Scope of Culinary Information and Seasonal Terroir

All descriptions, sample menus, ingredient notes, and botanical infusion descriptions published across sunsettablego.com, our journal chronicles, and promotional literature are provided for general educational, illustrative, and culinary appreciation purposes. Because our dining gatherings rely exclusively on fresh seasonal harvests from local regenerative farms and wild-caught ocean fisheries, menu components, garnishes, and specific course presentations may vary based on daily harvest yields, morning weather conditions, and seasonal microclimate factors.

2. Open-Air Dining Environments and Natural Elements

Dining alfresco in natural wilderness and coastal environments involves natural atmospheric elements that differ significantly from enclosed indoor restaurants. Guests attending SunsetTableGo gatherings should take note of the following environmental factors:

  • Temperature Fluctuations: As the sun sets, ambient temperatures along coastal bluffs and mountain valleys drop rapidly. While we provide heated soapstone footrests, radiant hearth fires, and wool blankets, guests are advised to dress in warm, comfortable layers.
  • Natural Terrain and Footwear: Our outdoor long tables are situated on natural ground surfaces—including coastal grass bluffs, vineyard soils, and olive grove terraces. We strongly advise wearing flat, sturdy, comfortable footwear rather than high heels or delicate shoes.
  • Insects and Wildlife: Natural open-air environments may contain flying insects, ocean breezes, and regional wildlife. We utilize natural botanical citronella candles and organic lavender diffusers around our dining perimeters to ensure guest comfort.
  • Sunlight and Glare: Pre-sunset golden hour light can produce low-angle glare. Guests are welcome to bring polarized sunglasses or wide-brimmed hats for the initial seating phase.

3. Food Allergens and Dietary Precautions

While our culinary team takes rigorous precautions when preparing specialized meals for guests with disclosed food allergies, our mobile kitchens operate in open-air natural environments where ingredients are prepared in close proximity. We cannot guarantee absolute zero cross-contact with common food allergens, including tree nuts, seeds, dairy, eggs, soy, and shellfish. Guests with severe or life-threatening anaphylactic allergies must carry their personal emergency epinephrine auto-injectors and notify our staff upon arrival.

4. Open-Flame Hearth Safety

Our cooking stations utilize live wood embers, open flame grills, and heavy cast-iron cookware operating at temperatures exceeding 400°C. Guests are requested to maintain a safe distance from designated hearth zones and adhere to all safety boundaries established by our culinary and event staff. Children must be supervised by a parent or guardian at all times when in proximity to open cooking fires.

5. Health and Wellness Guidance

Nothing contained on this website or in our botanical beverage descriptions constitutes medical advice or specialized clinical health claims. Our botanical infusions, herbal teas, and cold-pressed elixirs are crafted solely for sensory enjoyment and culinary pairing. Individuals with specific medical conditions or dietary sensitivities should consult their personal healthcare providers regarding herbal ingredients.

6. Natural Terrain Navigation and Evening Mobility

Because our nomadic gatherings take place across pristine natural landscapes—including coastal cliffpaths, terraced olive groves, and mountain overlooks—guests will navigate uneven natural terrain, unpaved gravel trails, and gentle slopes during arrival and departure. We provide low-level solar ground path lighting and escorted host assistance along all primary pathways. Guests requiring specialized mobility assistance or step-free access are warmly encouraged to notify our concierge team during the reservation process so that custom seating and vehicle drop-off access can be seamlessly arranged.

7. Seasonal Insect Management and Natural Aromatics

In outdoor garden and coastal environments, evening insect activity varies with seasonal temperatures and humidity. SunsetTableGo utilizes natural, non-toxic botanical aromatics—including pure steam-distilled citronella, eucalyptus, and wild lavender hydrosol diffusers—placed discreetly around our seating perimeters to ensure a tranquil dining atmosphere without the use of harsh synthetic chemical sprays or offensive odors.
