Digital Preferences & Cookie Governance

Cookie Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 • SunsetTableGo Digital Services

1. Introduction to Cookie Technologies

This Cookie Policy explains how SunsetTableGo ("Organization," "we," "us," or "our") uses cookies, pixel tags, local browser storage objects, and similar digital tracking technologies when you navigate our website sunsettablego.com, access our interactive Sunset Table Configurator, or read our culinary research chronicles. We utilize these tools to ensure seamless site functionality, enhance user navigation speeds, remember your custom dining configuration presets, and analyze aggregate visitor traffic patterns.

2. What Are Cookies?

A cookie is a small alphanumeric text file placed on your computer, tablet, or smartphone by a website's server when you visit a web page. Cookies enable the website to recognize your device upon return visits, remember your interactive preferences (such as selected landscape settings or dietary preferences), and provide an efficient, personalized browsing experience. Cookies may be "session cookies" (which are automatically erased when you close your browser) or "persistent cookies" (which remain stored on your device until they expire or are manually deleted).

3. Categories of Cookies Deployed on Our Platform

We classify the cookies utilized on sunsettablego.com into three distinct functional categories:

A. Strictly Necessary (Essential) Cookies

These cookies are fundamentally required for the technical operation of our platform. They enable secure user navigation between pages, facilitate session authentication, and ensure that items saved in your reservation or configurator forms persist across page refreshes. Without these cookies, our core digital services cannot be delivered. These cookies do not collect personal records for marketing purposes and cannot be deactivated in our systems.

B. Performance and Analytical Cookies

These cookies collect aggregated, pseudonymous telemetry regarding how visitors interact with our digital repository. They help us identify which culinary research articles are most frequently read, calculate average dwell time across dining calendar tables, measure bounce rates, and diagnose page rendering errors. We utilize Google Analytics (tag ID: G-0LY0HY7L01) to gather these operational insights. All collected analytics data is aggregated and anonymized, preventing individual personal identification.

C. Functionality and Preference Cookies

These cookies allow our website to remember choices you make while browsing—such as your preferred dining landscape filters, previously selected banquet setups, and saved configurator mockups. These cookies provide an elevated, tailored user interface during subsequent visits.

4. Third-Party Tracking Technologies

In addition to our first-party cookies, certain trusted third-party analytics and infrastructure partners may place cookies on your device when you browse our site. These third parties operate under their respective privacy policies and contractual data processing agreements with SunsetTableGo, ensuring high standards of cybersecurity and data confidentiality.

5. Managing and Disabling Cookies

You maintain complete control over how cookies are utilized on your device. Most modern web browsers provide settings that allow you to view stored cookies, delete existing cookies, block third-party cookies, or reject cookies entirely. Please consult your browser's official documentation for instructions:

  • Google Chrome: Settings → Privacy and Security → Cookies and other site data
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Apple Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings → Cookies and site permissions

Please note that if you elect to disable or block essential cookies, certain interactive components of our platform—including the real-time Sunset Table Configurator—may not function as intended.

6. Updates to This Cookie Policy

We may periodically modify this Cookie Policy to reflect changes in web technologies, regulatory requirements, or our digital feature sets. Any updates will be posted directly to this page with an amended Effective Date.
